## Pretrained model (not written or trained by the team)

**YOLOv4-tiny, COCO weights** (`models/yolov4-tiny/yolov4-tiny.cfg`,
`yolov4-tiny.weights`), from the Darknet project by Alexey Bochkovskiy,
Chien-Yao Wang and Hong-Yuan Mark Liao, released under Darknet's own
"YOLO LICENSE" (free to use, no warranty). COCO, the dataset it was trained
on, is published under CC BY 4.0. The team only runs inference (class
"person") through OpenCV DNN; no weights were changed.

be regenerated offline. To obtain them independently: they are the two
"yolov4-tiny" release files of AlexeyAB's Darknet repository (search for
"darknet yolov4-tiny.weights"); check the hashes above after download.
